Beat Em Up

Support

Last updated 22 November 2026

Beat Em Up is a fighting game for iPhone and iPad for one to four players. It is rated 18+.

Get help or report a problem

Write to t-innovations@outlook.com. Say what happened and when. We read messages at least once a week and answer within seven days.

Anyone can report a problem with online play here, whether or not they play the game. In the game, the same is under Settings → Safety and privacy → Report a problem, in the pause menu during an online game and in the online lobby.

If someone is in danger, call the emergency services first (999 in the United Kingdom).

Online play in short

  • Up to four players fight the game's enemies together, through Apple's Game Center.
  • There is no text chat and no voice chat. Players see each other's Game Center nickname; anyone can hide all names in the game under Settings → Safety and privacy.
  • An offensive name? Tell us the name exactly as it was shown and we will hide it for everybody.
  • You can leave an online game at any time from the pause menu, and report or block a player in Game Center.
  • The game collects no personal data.

Common questions

The game says it needs an age check, or that it is for adults only. The game is rated 18+ and asks Apple for your age range. Age ranges are managed in the Settings app: your name, then Personal Information, then Age Range for Apps. Apple explains it here: https://support.apple.com/122770

Online play does not start. Check that you are signed in to Game Center (Settings app, Game Center) and that Screen Time allows multiplayer games.

The music. Settings → Music and soundtrack has a switch and five soundtracks to choose from. The same is in the pause menu.

A refund. Purchases are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
