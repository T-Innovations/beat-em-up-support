Who can play

Beat Em Up shows realistic fighting and is rated 18+. It is for adults.

Where Apple tells the game that the player is under 18, the game does not start.

How online play works

Up to four players fight the game's enemies together. You can invite Game Center friends, be matched with other players automatically, or play with people nearby. Sign-in, friends, invitations and matching are run by Apple's Game Center.

Players cannot talk or write to each other in the game. There is no text chat, no voice chat, no pictures and no links. All that other players see of you is your Game Center nickname, the fighter you chose and what your fighter does.

You can hide all player names under Settings, Safety and privacy. Then your own nickname is not sent either, and players appear as Player 1 to Player 4.

Players cannot hurt each other's fighters. You can leave an online game at any time from the pause menu.

What is not allowed

  • Do not use the game, or any changed copy of it, to harass, threaten, bully or abuse another person.
  • Your Game Center nickname is shown to other players. Do not use a nickname that is offensive, hateful, sexual or threatening, that pretends to be someone else, or that carries a message, an address or a link.
  • Do not try to send or show other players any other words, pictures, sounds or links through the game.
  • Illegal content of every kind is prohibited. So is pornographic content, and content that encourages, promotes or gives instructions for suicide, self-injury or eating disorders. This applies to every player.
  • Do not cheat, and do not interfere with the game or with other players' connections.

How players are protected

The main protection is the way the game is built: apart from a nickname it gives players no means of communicating with each other, and it stores nothing that players do.

We do not use automated tools to scan or detect content.

Reports are read by a person. If a nickname breaks these terms we can hide it for all players, usually within a day, and we can hide all names or switch online play off altogether. We cannot see live games and we cannot ban a player, because sign-in and matching belong to Apple. We can also pass a report to Apple, to the police or to another authority.

You can hide all player names yourself, and you can report or block a player in Apple's Game Center.

How to report a problem or complain

Use "Report a problem" in the game (in Settings, in the pause menu during an online game and in the online lobby), or write to t-innovations@outlook.com. Anyone may report, whether or not they play the game.

Tell us what happened and when. If it is about a name, tell us the name exactly as it was shown. You can report something you believe is illegal or harmful, complain that we are not doing what these terms or the law require, or complain about how we handled an earlier report, including a decision to hide your own nickname.

We read reports at least once a week and answer within seven days. If you disagree with our answer, write again and we will look at it once more.

If someone is in danger, call the emergency services first (999 in the United Kingdom).
